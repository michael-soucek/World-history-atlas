Napoleon Bonaparte was a French military leader who rose to prominence during the French Revolution, seized power in 1799 and crowned himself Emperor of the French in 1804. His armies conquered much of Europe, and his reforms, especially the Napoleonic Code, transformed law and government across the continent. Defeated in 1814 and again at Waterloo in 1815, he died in exile.

## Corsica

Napoleon was born on 15 August 1769 in Ajaccio, Corsica, a year after France acquired the island from Genoa, into a family of minor nobility. He was educated at military schools in France and became an artillery officer in 1785. He was a supporter of the French Revolution.

## Rise

In 1793, at the siege of Toulon, where royalists had admitted a British fleet, Napoleon's artillery plan forced the British out, and he was promoted to brigadier general at twenty-four. In 1795 he dispersed a royalist uprising in Paris with cannon fire, "a whiff of grapeshot". In 1796 he married Joséphine de Beauharnais and was given command of the army of Italy, where he won a series of brilliant victories against the Austrians, at Lodi, Arcole and Rivoli, and created republics in northern Italy. In 1798 he invaded Egypt, defeating the Mamluks at the Battle of the Pyramids, but Nelson destroyed his fleet at the Nile. Scholars who accompanied him found the Rosetta Stone.

## Consul

Napoleon returned to France and, on 18 Brumaire, 9 November 1799, took part in a coup that overthrew the Directory, becoming First Consul. He defeated Austria at Marengo in 1800 and made peace. At home, he restored order, reformed administration, created the Bank of France and the lycées, made peace with the Catholic Church through the Concordat of 1801, and in 1804 issued the Civil Code, the Napoleonic Code, which established equality before the law, secular authority and property rights, while restricting women's rights. He restored slavery in the French colonies in 1802 and failed to retake Haiti.

## Emperor

On 2 December 1804, in Notre-Dame, Napoleon crowned himself emperor. In 1805 the British under Nelson destroyed the French and Spanish fleets at Trafalgar, but Napoleon crushed the Austrians and Russians at Austerlitz. He defeated Prussia at Jena in 1806 and Russia at Friedland in 1807. He dissolved the Holy Roman Empire, made his brothers kings, and imposed the Continental System, a blockade of British trade. In 1810 he divorced Joséphine, who had no children, and married the Austrian archduchess Marie Louise, who bore him a son.

## Spain and Russia

In 1808 Napoleon placed his brother Joseph on the Spanish throne, provoking a long guerrilla war, supported by the British. In 1812 he invaded Russia with over 600,000 men. He took Moscow after the bloody battle of Borodino, but the city burned and the Russians refused to negotiate. During the retreat through the Russian winter, most of his army was lost.

## Fall

A coalition of European powers defeated Napoleon at Leipzig in 1813, the "Battle of the Nations", and took Paris in 1814. He abdicated and was exiled to the island of Elba. In March 1815 he escaped and returned to power for the Hundred Days, until his defeat by Wellington and Blücher at Waterloo on 18 June 1815. He was exiled to Saint Helena, in the South Atlantic, where he died on 5 May 1821. His remains were returned to Paris in 1840.

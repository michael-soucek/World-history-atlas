Otto von Bismarck was a Prussian statesman who unified Germany in 1871 and served as its first chancellor until 1890. Through three wars and skilful diplomacy, he created the German Empire, then worked to preserve peace in Europe through a complex system of alliances. At home, he introduced the world's first comprehensive social insurance system.

## Junker

Bismarck was born on 1 April 1815 at Schönhausen, in Brandenburg, into a family of Prussian landowning nobles, Junkers. He studied law, worked briefly as a civil servant and managed his family estates. A deeply conservative monarchist, he entered politics in 1847 and opposed the revolutions of 1848. He served as Prussia's representative to the German Confederation in Frankfurt and as ambassador to Russia and France.

## Minister-president

In 1862 King Wilhelm I, in a conflict with the Prussian parliament over army reform, appointed Bismarck as minister-president. In a famous speech, he declared that the great questions of the day would be decided not by speeches and majority decisions but by "iron and blood". He governed for four years without an approved budget, collecting taxes and reforming the army.

## Three wars

Bismarck used war and diplomacy to unite Germany under Prussian leadership. In 1864 Prussia and Austria defeated Denmark, taking Schleswig and Holstein. In 1866 Prussia defeated Austria at Königgrätz in the Seven Weeks' War, excluding Austria from German affairs; Bismarck insisted on lenient terms for Austria. Prussia annexed several states and formed the North German Confederation. In 1870 Bismarck edited a telegram from the king about a meeting with the French ambassador at Ems to make it seem insulting, provoking France to declare war. The southern German states joined Prussia. The Germans defeated France and captured Napoleon III at Sedan. On 18 January 1871, in the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles, Wilhelm was proclaimed German emperor, and Bismarck became chancellor. Germany annexed Alsace-Lorraine.

## Keeping the peace

As chancellor, Bismarck declared Germany satisfied and sought to keep France isolated. He formed the League of the Three Emperors with Austria-Hungary and Russia, the Dual Alliance with Austria-Hungary in 1879, the Triple Alliance with Italy in 1882 and the Reinsurance Treaty with Russia in 1887. At the Congress of Berlin in 1878, he presented himself as an "honest broker" settling the Balkan crisis. He hosted the Berlin Conference of 1884–1885, which set rules for European colonisation of Africa, and Germany acquired colonies.

## At home

Bismarck fought the Catholic Church in the Kulturkampf, the "culture struggle", in the 1870s, expelling the Jesuits and imposing state control of education and marriage, but later made peace with the Catholic Centre Party. He banned the Social Democratic Party under the Anti-Socialist Laws of 1878. At the same time, to undermine socialism, he introduced health insurance in 1883, accident insurance in 1884 and old-age and disability pensions in 1889, the first such system in the world.

## Dismissal

In 1888 the young Wilhelm II became emperor. He clashed with Bismarck and forced his resignation in March 1890. Bismarck retired to his estate at Friedrichsruh, where he wrote memoirs and criticised the government. He died on 30 July 1898.

Islam is a monotheistic religion founded in the seventh century in Arabia, based on the teachings of the Prophet Muhammad and the Quran, which Muslims believe to be the word of God, Allah, revealed to Muhammad. With nearly two billion followers, it is the world's second-largest religion.

## Beliefs

Muslims believe in one God, who is unique and has no partners, and that Muhammad is the last of a line of prophets that includes Abraham, Moses and Jesus. The word Islam means "submission" to God, and a Muslim is "one who submits". Muslims believe in angels, revealed scriptures, including the Torah and the Gospel as well as the Quran, the prophets, the Day of Judgement and God's will.

## The Five Pillars

Muslim practice is based on the Five Pillars: the declaration of faith, the shahada, "There is no god but God, and Muhammad is the messenger of God"; prayer, salat, five times a day, facing Mecca; almsgiving, zakat, to the poor; fasting, sawm, from dawn to sunset during the month of Ramadan; and pilgrimage, hajj, to Mecca, at least once in a lifetime for those who are able.

## Muhammad and the Quran

Muhammad was born in Mecca around 570. According to Islamic tradition, from about 610 he received revelations from God through the angel Gabriel. Facing opposition in Mecca, he and his followers migrated to Medina in 622, the Hijra, the beginning of the Islamic calendar. By his death in 632, most of Arabia had accepted Islam. The revelations were compiled into the Quran, written in Arabic, under the third caliph, Uthman. The hadith, reports of Muhammad's sayings and actions, and the sunna, his example, are also sources of guidance. Islamic law, sharia, is derived from these sources by scholars.

## Sunni and Shia

After Muhammad's death, disagreements arose over leadership. The majority, Sunnis, accepted the first caliphs, Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman and Ali. Shia Muslims believe that leadership should have passed to Ali, Muhammad's cousin and son-in-law, and his descendants, the imams. The killing of Ali's son Husayn at Karbala in 680 is commemorated by Shia Muslims during Ashura. Sunnis make up about 85 to 90 percent of Muslims. Sufism is a mystical tradition within Islam.

## Expansion

Under the early caliphs and the Umayyad dynasty, Arab armies conquered a vast empire, from Spain to Central Asia, within a century. Islam spread further through trade, missionaries and Sufi orders, to West and East Africa, South Asia, South-East Asia and Central Asia. Indonesia today has the largest Muslim population.

## Civilisation

Under the Abbasid caliphate and other states, Islamic civilisation flourished in science, mathematics, medicine, philosophy, art and architecture, in the Islamic Golden Age. Great mosques, such as the Dome of the Rock, the Great Mosque of Córdoba and later the Taj Mahal and Süleymaniye Mosque, were built. Great empires, such as the Ottoman, Safavid and Mughal, ruled much of the Muslim world in the early modern period.

## Today

Muslims live in every part of the world, with large communities in the Middle East, Africa, South Asia, Southeast Asia, Europe and North America. Mecca and Medina, in Saudi Arabia, and Jerusalem are its holiest cities.
